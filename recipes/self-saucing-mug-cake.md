# Self-Saucing Chocolate Mug Cake

A decadent, gooey chocolate cake that cooks into its own rich sauce in just 90 seconds.

## Ingredients

### The Cake Base
- 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp cocoa powder (unsweetened)
- 2 tbsp sugar (granulated or brown)
- 1/4 tsp baking powder
- 1/8 tsp salt
- 3 tbsp milk (or dairy-free alternative)
- 1 tbsp melted butter (or coconut oil)
- 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
- A pinch of instant coffee (optional, to enhance chocolate)

### The Sauce Core
- 1 tbsp chocolate chips or chopped dark chocolate
- 1 tsp peanut butter (or almond butter)
- 1 tsp heavy cream (or coconut milk)

## Instructions

1. **Mix the Base:** In a large microwave-safe mug (at least 12oz/350ml), whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, sugar, baking powder, and salt.
2. **Add Wet Ingredients:** Stir in the milk, melted butter, vanilla extract, and coffee. Mix until just combined (a few lumps are okay).
3. **Create the Well:** Use a spoon to make a small well (about 1 inch wide) in the center of the batter. 
4. **Add the Sauce:** Drop the chocolate chips, peanut butter, and heavy cream into the well. Do not stir them into the batter; just nestle them in.
5. **Microwave:** Cook on high for 90 seconds to 2 minutes. (Time varies by microwave wattage—start with 90s and check for a firm top and gooey center).
6. **Serve:** Let sit for 1 minute to allow the sauce to thicken slightly. Eat directly from the mug!

*Tip: For a richer sauce, press a square of dark chocolate into the center well.*
