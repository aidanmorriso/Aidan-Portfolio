Self-Saucing Chocolate Mug Cake yum

A gooey chocolate cake that cooks into its own sauce in 90 seconds. Microwave punk rock.

The Cake Base

  • 4 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1/4 tsp baking powder
  • 1/8 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 1 tbsp melted butter
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla
  • Pinch of instant coffee (optional)

The Sauce Core

  • 1 tbsp chocolate chips / dark chocolate
  • 1 tsp peanut butter
  • 1 tsp heavy cream

Method

  1. Whisk the dry ingredients in a big mug (350ml+).
  2. Stir in milk, butter, vanilla and coffee until just combined.
  3. Make a 1-inch well in the centre.
  4. Drop the chocolate, peanut butter and cream into the well — don't stir.
  5. Microwave on high 90s–2min, until the top is firm and the centre gooey.
  6. Rest 1 minute, then eat straight from the mug.

tip: shove a square of dark chocolate in the well for extra sauce.
