2012 VW Golf Mk7 TSI

1.4L Turbo · 6MT · RHD

Mk7 Golf in 1.4 TSI guise — the EA211 1.4L turbo four, reined in with a proper 6-speed manual. Right-hand drive conversion, so it's at home on NZ roads. Light, tossable, and quick enough to surprise people who think it's just a Golf.

Why It's Here

The Mk7 was the last truly light Golf. Under 1,300 kg with a manual box makes it a proper driver's car — not a tech-laden appliance. The RHD conversion puts the gear lever in the right hand where it belongs.
