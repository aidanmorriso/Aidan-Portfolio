From: Aidan Morrison
To: Automotive Enthusiasts

Subject: My Garage & Machines

I'm a firm believer that if you can't build it yourself, you don't truly understand how it works. My collection isn't just about the speed; it's about the engineering, the maintenance, and the constant pursuit of perfecting the drive. From classic restorations to modern performance builds, every machine in my garage tells a story of grease, sweat, and technical precision.

Whether I'm tuning a carburetor or diagnostic-testing a modern ECU, the goal is the same: peak performance through meticulous attention to detail. Welcome to my garage.
